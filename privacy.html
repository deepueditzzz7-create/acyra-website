PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how ACYRA Private Limited may collect, use, protect and manage information provided through its website and related digital interactions.

01

Introduction

ACYRA Private Limited ("ACYRA", "we", "our" or "us") respects the privacy of individuals who visit our website, contact us, apply for opportunities, use our services or otherwise interact with us.

This Privacy Policy describes the general approach ACYRA may follow when handling personal information collected through its website and related services.

By using the website or submitting information through our forms, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with ACYRA, we may collect information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Information You Provide

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Company or organisation information
  • Recruitment and employment-related information
  • Resume, CV and professional information
  • Training or program-related information
  • Enquiry details and messages submitted through website forms
  • Information you voluntarily provide during communication with ACYRA

Technical Information

Depending on the website configuration, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as browser type, device information, IP address, approximate usage information, pages visited and technical logs.

The exact categories collected will depend on the technologies and services enabled on the production website.

03

How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate business, service and communication purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests
  • Providing and improving IT solutions
  • Understanding product requirements
  • Supporting recruitment and staffing activities
  • Processing career applications
  • Delivering training and placement-related services
  • Communicating about relevant services or opportunities
  • Maintaining website functionality and security
  • Detecting, preventing and investigating misuse, fraud or security incidents
  • Complying with applicable legal or regulatory requirements
Data minimisation

ACYRA should collect only information reasonably necessary for the stated purpose and avoid requesting unnecessary sensitive information through public website forms.

04

Information Sharing

ACYRA does not intend to sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary for providing services, operating the website, processing applications or fulfilling legal obligations.

Service Providers

Depending on the production architecture, ACYRA may use trusted third-party providers for services such as hosting, email delivery, analytics, security, communications, cloud infrastructure and application functionality.

Legal Requirements

Information may be disclosed where required or permitted by applicable law, regulation, court order, government request or where necessary to protect legitimate rights, safety or security.

05

Data Security

ACYRA should implement reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, misuse or destruction.

Depending on the final system architecture, security controls may include:

  • HTTPS and encrypted data transmission
  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Password hashing for user accounts
  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Input validation and sanitisation
  • Protection against common web vulnerabilities
  • Security monitoring and logging
  • Dependency and vulnerability management
  • Secure server and database configuration

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid submitting passwords, financial credentials or other highly sensitive information through general enquiry forms unless specifically requested through a secure process.

06

Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, meet contractual or business needs, resolve disputes, maintain records or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods depending on their purpose and legal requirements.

07

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the processing, individuals may have rights relating to their personal information.

These may include requesting access, correction, updating, deletion or withdrawal of certain forms of consent where applicable.

Requests should be submitted using the contact details provided on the final production website.

Data deletion requests

ACYRA should provide an appropriate mechanism for individuals to request deletion or removal of personal information, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

08

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The ACYRA website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential website functionality, remember preferences, improve performance and, where enabled, understand website usage.

The production website should clearly identify non-essential cookies and provide an appropriate consent mechanism where required.

Users may also be able to control cookies through their browser settings.

A separate Cookie Policy should be provided once the final analytics, advertising and third-party tracking technologies are selected.

09

Third-Party Services

The website may contain integrations or links to third-party services, platforms or websites.

Examples may include hosting providers, analytics platforms, communication services, payment providers, recruitment platforms, social media services and other technology providers.

Third-party services operate according to their own privacy policies and terms. ACYRA should review every third-party SDK, script, API and integration before it is enabled on the production website.

10

Children's Privacy

The website is not intended to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children.

Where training or educational services may involve students under the applicable age of consent, the production implementation should include appropriate consent, safeguarding and data-handling procedures.

11

Changes to This Policy

ACYRA may update this Privacy Policy when its services, technology, legal requirements or data-processing practices change.

When changes are made, the updated policy should be published on this page together with an updated effective or revision date.

12

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, personal information or a data-related request, please contact ACYRA through the official contact channel provided on the website.
